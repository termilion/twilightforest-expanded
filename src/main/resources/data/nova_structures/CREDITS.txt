The files in data/nova_structures are taken from the "Dungeons and Taverns" datapack (namespace nova_structures)
by Nova Wostra (lead developer, structure builds and placement), Walls (loot tables and enchantments),
Konci, Badooga and Whity Lee.

Imported: undead_crypt templates, template pools, processor list and loot tables.
Changes: enchanted books in chests/undead_crypts_grave draw from #minecraft:on_random_loot instead of
#nova_structures:shrine (the pack's custom enchantments are not included). Placement and height are defined by
twilightforest:undead_crypt.
chests/undead_crypts_grave also got a pool with a full roll of minecraft:chests/end_city_treasure in 1 of 5 chests
and a pool with an elytra in 1 of 100 chests. The remnant graveyard uses twilightforest:chests/spooky_graveyard
(the grave loot without these two pools) instead.
worldgen/template_pool/undead_crypt_mob was rewritten to use Twilight Forest mobs and spawners
(templates in data/twilightforest/structure/undead_crypt); the original skeleton/zombie/spider mob and spawner
templates were removed. The keep skeleton templates copy the skeletons of the ATi Structures dark_keep.
The central spawners of undead_crypt_spawner_room_1..3 now spawn death tomes, minotaurs, king spiders and strays.
The main pool of chests/undead_crypts_grave additionally contains inline copies of twilightforest:stronghold_cache,
stronghold_room and darktower_cache reduced to their Twilight Forest items (3/4 of the chests); the original crypt
loot keeps 1/4. The copies do not follow later changes of the Twilight Forest tables.

Also imported for the spooky forest ruins (twilightforest:spooky_*): remnant_graveyard, remnant_woodland_hud,
wild_ruin and firewatch_tower_dark_oak with their pools, processors and loot. Loot tables ruin_loot_master, pot_wild_ruin
and firewatch_tower were wrapped: the original loot fills 1 in 7 containers, Twilight Forest tables (vanilla entries
emptied) the rest; #nova_structures:traveler enchantments replaced by #minecraft:on_random_loot.

lone_citadel (2026-10-13): templates, template pools, processor list citadel_degradation, pillar features (*_base), block tag support_goes_through
and loot tables (chests/pots/spawners/equipment/spawner_projectile lone_citadel, keys citadel_key / citadel_boss_key) copied from Dungeons and Taverns
and modified (amethyst/blackstone re-skin, Twilight Forest mobs and loot).

ruin_town (2026-10-14): templates (structure/ruin_town), template pools ruin_town*, processor lists (ruined_town_degradation / ruined_town_road_degradation,
copied as amethyst_ruin_town_* with a blackstone/deepslate/canopy/darkwood block swap), loot tables pots/pot_generic_ruin and
minecraft:archaelogy/ruins copied from Dungeons and Taverns. Missing chests/ruin_loot_1..7 references retargeted to chests/ruin_loot_master.

remnant/woodland_hut (2026-10-15): a persistent twilightforest:brownie was added to the template's entities (inside the hut).

underground_house (2026-10-15): templates underground_house_1..5 / _trap_1..5, pool underground_house, the witch_hut/foundation pool and
witch_hut_base feature (renamed nova_structures:underground_house_foundation / underground_house_base). Loot replaced by Twilight Forest lich tower
tables (dungeon_1 tower_room, 2 tower_foyer, 3 tower_library, 4 tower_potion, 5 tower_enchanting, pots tower_jars); one persistent brownie per house.
