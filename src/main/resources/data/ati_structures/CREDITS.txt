The files in data/ati_structures are taken from the "ATi Structures" datapack (namespace ati_structures).
The datapack ships without author or license information; rights must be clarified before distribution.

Imported: dark_keep (template, pool, loot tables it references) and its terrain_small foundation.
Used as the surface entrance of twilightforest:undead_crypt, connected by twilightforest's spiral staircase piece.

Also imported for the spooky forest ruins: haunted_ruin (3 variants, TNT trap kept) and rotten_log. The haunted ruin
chests now use ati_structures:chests/haunted_ruin (1 in 7 minecraft:chests/simple_dungeon, otherwise Twilight Forest
graveyard/hill_1 loot with vanilla entries emptied).

Also imported as oak savannah landmarks (twilightforest:savanna_*): quarry, gnome_hut (2 variants) and ati_stoneworks with
the stoneworks pieces and terrain_large foundations. Changes: all loot containers use Twilight Forest hollow hill tables
(gnome hut hill_1, quarry and stoneworks pieces hill_2, stoneworks main building hill_3); all hostile mobs replaced by
mad dwarves (5), mad dwarf warriors (3) and kobolds or cave spiders (2), about 1 in 20 of them a vex; the terrain pool now points at terrain_large/terrain1..2 (the original
pointed at a non-existing terrain/ folder).

Also imported for the darkwood clearing landmark (twilightforest:arachnid_dwelling): arachnid_dwelling (template + pool). Wood blocks
swapped to Twilight Forest darkwood (bookshelf → canopy bookshelf), spiders made persistent, chests retargeted to
twilightforest:chests/arachnid_dwelling/* = the original ATi pools + Lich Tower and woodland mansion loot.

2026-10-16: ruined_castle (template + pool, loot stone_storage, potion_storage, archer_chest2, armory; armory trident enchant level fixed)
for twilightforest:highland_ruined_castle. rotten_log and haunted_ruin are also used by twilightforest:{canopy,darkwood}_rotten_log and
darkwood_haunted_ruin. Wood is swapped by processors (twilightforest:block_swap) to the wood of the biome; templates unchanged.
